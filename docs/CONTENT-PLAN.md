# Marren - Content Plan

> Sitemap plus section-by-section copy for every page, written in Marren's voice.
> This is the placeholder content the build ships with. It reads like a real brand so the site can be evaluated honestly, and every string is meant to be reviewed and replaced with final copy.

## Voice

Warm, tactile, curatorial, and quietly confident.
We talk about materials and moments, not features and funnels.
Sentence case for headlines. No exclamation-mark hype. Short sentences. Concrete nouns.

Brand line: **Frames worth the wall.**

### FAQ (accordion)
- How do I know what size to order? Measure your print or photo, then match the frame size. Our sizing guide walks you through mats and borders.
- Are the frames ready to hang? Yes. Every frame ships with hanging hardware and, for sets, a paper template.
- What glass do you use? Shatter-resistant, low-glare glazing on every frame.
- Where does the wood come from? FSC-certified suppliers, finished in small batches.
- What is your return policy? 30 days, no fuss. Frames are covered for life against defects.
- How fast is shipping? Most orders leave within one business day; free over $75.

### Value proposition
- Heading: Everything a good gallery wall needs, and nothing it does not.
- Three to four points:
  - Curated combinations: sizes and finishes chosen to balance on a wall.
  - Hanging template included: tape it up, mark, hang. Straight, first try.
  - One material family: cohesive, never mismatched.
  - Ships ready: hardware in the box, frames finished and protected.

### Visual proof
- Heading: See it on a wall.
- Three example arrangements (Salon Six, Corridor, Trio) with piece counts and prices.

### Social proof (focused)
- One or two strong quotes about the sets specifically (reuse Daniel R. quote plus one more).

### Objection-handling FAQ
- Will it fit my wall? Each set lists total dimensions and comes with a template to test the layout before you commit.
- What if a frame arrives damaged? We replace it, no questions, and every frame carries a lifetime guarantee.
- Can I mix sizes later? Yes. Buy singles anytime to extend the wall.
- How hard is hanging? If you can use painter's tape and a pencil, you can do this.
